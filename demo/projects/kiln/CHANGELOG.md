# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

## [0.4.0] - 2026-09-22

### Added

- Gaps of more than ten minutes between readings are reported as anomalies.

### Changed

- Segments are classified as heating, holding or cooling by rate rather than by temperature band.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-09-08

### Added

- `kiln report` prints a plain-text summary of one firing.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-08-27

### Added

- Spike detection for thermocouple glitches.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-15

### Added

- Reading controller CSV logs into timestamped readings.
